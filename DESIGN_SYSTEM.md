# Design system

Warm paper #FAFAF8, graphite #111111, muted grey #777777, cobalt #4667DA. Dark mode #0B0B0C with #F4F4F4 text. Generous whitespace, thin dividers, rounded cards, readable typography.

Four primary surfaces: Notes, Timeline, Garden, Search. Notes opens first. A small breathing orb signals the assistant, recording intensity reflects metering, and visible text labels accompany actions. Touch targets at least 44 px. Honor reduced motion. No forced onboarding or login.

Greeting defaults to once per local calendar day and can be disabled. Permission and inference state are visible. Empty states explain the next action without manufactured content.
